Hi, I'm Florian and this is my **Product Management Playbook**

![PM Playbook](/images/pm-playbook-cover.jpg)

This site is a curated collection of:
- Foundations
- Frameworks
- Templates
- Case studies
- Tools & resources

Built for product managers who want clarity, not noise.

This repository exists to:

- Share battle-tested PM templates and frameworks
- Document best practices across the product lifecycle
- Showcase personal product projects and case studies
- Help junior PMs ramp up faster
- Give experienced PMs reusable assets
- Encourage open collaboration and discussion

Whether you are an Aspiring PM, Product Manager, Product Owner, or Founder, this repo is meant to save you time and sharpen your thinking.
